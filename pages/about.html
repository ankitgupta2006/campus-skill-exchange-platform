ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Learn from your
campus community.

Create a profile, discover useful skills, and connect with fellow students for peer-to-peer learning.

OUR PURPOSE

Turning students into teachers and learners.

Every student knows something valuable. One student may know programming while another may be excellent at photography, mathematics, communication or design.

Campus Skill Exchange provides a simple way to bring these students together and encourage peer-to-peer learning.

What students can do

  • Create a student profile
  • Add skills they can teach
  • Discover useful skills
  • Find students with similar interests
  • Build their campus network
